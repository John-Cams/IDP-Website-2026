Welcoming The First Light

In the heart of the B.I.O.M.E. habitat, a small but significant smell of toasted rye and fermentation wafts from the waste-reclamation labs. Meet Viktor Volkov, a systems specialist who has turned a hobby into a colony-wide tradition. Viktor is the colony’s primary producer of Mars-Kvass, a traditional Russian fermented beverage made from recycled hydroponic crusts.

Back on Earth, we looked at the sky. Here, we look at each other,' Viktor says, stirring a stainless steel vat. In a mission with 80 people isolated by the polar night, these small tastes of home provide more than just calories they provide psychological resilience. As we prepare for the First Light festival, Viktor’s brew serves as a reminder that even in the most sterile lab, human culture finds a way to ferment and flourish
